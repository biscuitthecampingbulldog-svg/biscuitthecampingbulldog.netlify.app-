🐾 Meet the star

Hi, I'm Biscuit!

I'm a certified service dog and English Bulldog living my best life full-time in the Great Smoky Mountains of Tennessee — rolling through every adventure in our Grand Design Reflection fifth wheel with my mom Denise and daddy Jeffrey. Home isn't a place for us, it's wherever we park for the night — a quiet hollow outside Cosby, a bluff overlooking the ridgelines, or a gravel site by a creek.

Every trail, campsite, and mountain sunrise is better with wrinkles and a pink camo bandana. Short legs, big heart, zero regrets. I've got a face made for snuggling and a nose built for finding every good smell within a mile of camp — campfires, creek banks, and other people's snacks are my specialties. 🏕️

Mom runs our brand from behind the camera while I handle the important stuff: exploring, drooling, and spreading joy one campsite at a time. Whether we're settling into a new site, testing out gear for the RV, or just parked by the water watching the world go by, I'm along for every mile.

This page is where we share it all — the gear that actually earns a spot in our RV, the wildlife we run into (from a very safe distance), and the everyday moments that make full-time life on the road with a bulldog worth it. Thanks for following along. 🐾
